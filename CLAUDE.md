# ECOES JV website

Static site (plain HTML + CSS + a little JS, no build step) for ECOES JV, built page by page from the client's PDFs in `source/`. Published by GitHub Pages from the branch `claude/website-build-files-m52uk7` (root): https://amg7899.github.io/SUM/

## Working with the client
- The client writes in Bulgarian (sometimes English or Russian); answer in the language of their message. The site is in English (root) and Bulgarian (`bg/`, our translation, added when the client asked that the Bulgarian always say "ECOES конверсия", never the browser's "преобразуване"/"преустройство"). The client reviews the Bulgarian; their wording wins.
- Use the client's text verbatim. Do not add copy, taglines or sections. Fix only obvious typos, and report every fix. When unsure, ask.
- After every change: commit, push to the branch above, and send the live link plus screenshots.
- Photos for the HISTORY section of About us come from https://bulfire.ecoes.eu/about-us/. That host is not reachable from the cloud sandbox, so ask the client to upload them. The client will come back to HISTORY once they have all the information.
- Publishing: GitHub Pages must be switched on by the client (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → this branch, / (root)); no tool here can do it. ecoes.eu currently answers with a certificate for another name (NET::ERR_CERT_COMMON_NAME_INVALID); moving the domain to GitHub Pages later needs DNS changes at the client's registrar.

## Design system (v2, "charged": dark, futuristic, translation-proof)
Subject: an engineering company converting diesel trucks, buses and agricultural/construction machinery to electric, plus a mobile-charging franchise. Audience: fleet owners, municipalities, bodybuilders, farmers, investors. The client asked for a futuristic, modern, professional look that still fits when the browser translates the page (v1, light "spec sheet", was rejected).

- Palette: void `#06090e` (page), hull `#0c1219` (plates), trace `rgba(148,178,210,.18)` (hairlines), ion `#edf2f8` (headings/emphasis), text `#c3cdd9`, mist `#93a1b3` (secondary), volt `#3db8ff` (energy: traces, focus, links, list markers), signal `#ff3344` (brand red: the € glyphs, current page, the solar-owners plate), paper `#fff` (plates that carry photos with white backgrounds).
- Type: Jost (display, 400–700; clean geometric, Futura lineage) + Geologica (text, 300–700, slnt for italics). Both have Cyrillic. Tektur was tried and rejected by the client as "childish, like a video game"; Jost approved.
- Shape: chamfered plates (top-right and bottom-left corners cut) with a 1px edge drawn along the cuts too; one shared rule in style.css (`.plate` and the component list next to it). Thin volt lines with a soft glow act as wiring: hero trace, About us schematic (trunk, bus, drops; rail with ticks on phones), section nodes on document pages.
- Home: hero with the company name small and "€CONOMY & €COLOGY" huge (wraps to two lines), the logo's globe drawn large and faint behind it, a trace that draws in once on load (the only non-user motion). Four tiles (plates) with the photo on a white inner plate, the PDF labels as chips above the photo, title + arrow below.
- Document pages: page head with grid texture and a short volt line under the title; ruled sections with a volt node, sticky heading column + text column ≥1024px; diamond list markers; callouts on a faint volt plate; the closing all-caps sentence as the big statement.
- Translation-proof rules: no nowrap on body text; containers grow with content; labels never sit on top of photos (chips wrap instead). Header: the menu button (☰) always sits left of the logo and opens every section full-screen; the row of links always stays on ONE line (client's rule): main.js scales its font and gaps down (to 74% at most) until it fits, re-checking on resize, font loads (`loadingdone`, a translation pulls in Cyrillic) and translation (`<html lang>`/class changes, link text changes); below 1024px, or if 74% is not enough, only the menu button remains. On English pages each link (and each "Get a quote" button) carries our Bulgarian name in `span.l10n-bg[lang=bg]` next to `span.l10n-src`, shown via `html[lang|=bg]` in the moment the browser translates to Bulgarian (main.js then moves on to the `bg/` page, see Languages). Brand names, the slogan, e-mails, phone numbers and addresses carry `translate="no"`, with the neighbouring spaces moved inside those spans (translation drops the space next to them otherwise: "ECOESпреустройство").
- Header menu (client's request): Home, About us, ECOES Conversion, FRANCHISE ECOES, Contact us, centred in the window (client: "center them … but not to the left"): the row is centred between the logo and the language button, and main.js moves it left (`--nav-shift`, a transform) to the window's middle as far as the free space allows while keeping 48px from the logo — exactly centred from about 1180px, between logo and button below that; "bus & trucks" and "agro & construction" sit in a dropdown under ECOES Conversion (the link itself still opens its page). With a mouse the panel slides down on hover; the arrow button next to the link opens it for touch and keyboard (`aria-expanded`); Escape, a click elsewhere or focus leaving closes it (Escape also hides a hover-opened panel until the mouse leaves). A volt wire drops from the link's underline to a chamfered panel with diamond markers; main.js shifts the panel left (`--sub-shift`) when it would leave the window. In the full-screen menu the two links sit under ECOES Conversion on a volt rail. On those two pages the parent link carries the red current-page mark (`.is-current`). The footer keeps all seven links.
- Test: pseudo-translate every page (Cyrillic, words ~45% longer) at 320/390/768/1024/1440 and check for horizontal overflow and clipped boxes (script used: Playwright, see git history of this note).
- Background drawings (client asked for faint ecology/future figures, patterns, symbols, diagrams): `assets/img/bg/eco-pattern.svg` (scattered Lucide symbols joined by dotted traces, behind the tiles and every page body at 13% opacity), `energy-flow.svg` (sun, wind, solar → battery → plug → truck, bottom-right of each page head at 30%; a dimmer copy in the full-screen menu), `eco-horizon.svg` (turbines, solar rows, pylons, trees, charger and an electric truck above the footer at 32%). Generated by a script from Lucide icons (ISC, licence in `assets/img/bg/LICENSE-lucide.txt`) plus hand-drawn turbines and pylons. The hero is unchanged (client likes it).
- Photos are being replaced by the client; image frames take any aspect ratio (the tile media flexes, figures are max 960px wide).
- Quality floor: contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible 2px volt focus ring, 44px targets, WebP + JPEG with width/height, no horizontal scroll at 320–1920px, reduced motion respected. clip-path also clips outlines, so chamfered controls draw their focus ring inside (`.btn`: dark inset ring; `.cta`/`.tile`: volt edge; `.nav-toggle`/`.lang-switch`: inset outline).

## Languages (English + Bulgarian)
- `bg/` mirrors every English page: `bg/index.html`, `bg/about-us/`, … with the same markup; only the text differs and asset paths have one more `../`. Every change to an English page must be made on its `bg/` twin too (and vice versa). Links inside `bg/` stay inside `bg/`.
- Terminology (client's rule): "конверсия" everywhere — "ECOES конверсия", "ECOES електрическа конверсия", "конверсия на автобуси и камиони", "селскостопанска и строителна техника"; never "преобразуване"/"преустройство". Also: "ФРАНЧАЙЗ ECOES", франчайзополучател, "вал за отбор на мощност (PTO)", надстройки / производители на надстройки, "Вземете оферта за …". Numbers in Bulgarian style (3,5; 3&nbsp;000; кг, км, об/мин), "EUR" kept. Company and product names stay in Latin (ECOES Ltd., BULFIRE ECOES Ltd., KINTEX SHC, AGRITRUCK, Night Charger, Solar Grid); cities in Bulgarian (Лондон, София, Истанбул); the Istanbul address and the London placeholders stay as written. Head office: бул. „Ал. Стамболийски“ 205, офис 320А, София 1309, България, ЕС. The CEO: д-р инж. Атанас Гушев.
- E-mail box (client's request): `address.header-mail` left of the language button, a chamfered frame of the same height (46px) with a volt envelope and ecoes@ecoes.eu / sales@ecoes.eu one above the other (mailto links, `translate="no"`). main.js hides it (`.mail-off`) when the row of links would not fit at full size with 48px on each side (it shows from about 1150px; at 1024–1150 the row gets the room); with the row off (480–1023px) it shows; below 480px it is hidden (CSS), the footer and Contact page carry the addresses. centerRow() centres the row between the logo and the right-hand group and then on the window, in either direction.
- Language switch: `.lang-switch` at the right end of the header, a frame the size of the menu button showing the other language (BG on English pages, EN on Bulgarian ones). The link to the current language stays in the markup (`aria-current`, hidden by CSS) because main.js reads the page language from it.
- main.js: when the browser translates a page into a language the site has (it sets `<html lang>`: Chrome English → Bulgarian, or Bulgarian → English), it goes to our version of the same page, keeping `?query` and `#hash`. A language chosen with the switch is kept for the tab (sessionStorage `ecoes-lang`); pages in that language then get `translate="no"`, so the browser does not translate them again. Translation into other languages is left alone.
- With `lang="bg"` Geologica uses the Bulgarian Cyrillic letterforms (OpenType locl: т, д, в, и closer to handwriting); Jost has none. The client saw these forms in the translated pages without objecting; standard forms would be `font-feature-settings: "locl" 0` on body (offered to the client).
- Header room: since the dropdown the row of links shows from 1024px in both languages; below that only the menu button.
- To add once the final domain is known: `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en|bg">` with absolute URLs on every page.

## Contact form, calls to action, footer
- Contact form on `contact-us/` and `bg/contact-us/` (`#contact-form`), handled by `assets/js/contact.js`: required name, e-mail, message and consent, errors shown next to each field, subject preselected from `?topic=` (conversion, bus-trucks, agro, franchise, other). Its messages follow `data-lang` on the form (en/bg). E-mail subject: "ECOES website: " + the chosen topic (in the page's language). If sending fails, the status says why — "not activated yet" when FormSubmit answers that the form needs activation, otherwise the general error plus a small "Details: …" line — and links to sales@ecoes.eu (cc bulfire@ecoes.eu) with the visitor's entries filled in, so nothing typed is lost. The form keeps its content after a failure. Sent through FormSubmit (no account): AJAX to `https://formsubmit.co/ajax/sales@ecoes.eu` with `_cc=bulfire@ecoes.eu`, `_subject`, `_template=table`, honeypot `_honey`; without JS it posts normally and comes back with `?sent=1`. FormSubmit needs a one-time activation: the first submission sends an "Activate Form" e-mail to sales@ecoes.eu. After activation FormSubmit offers a random alias to replace the address in the form action (hides it from scrapers) — swap it in when the client sends it.
- Every section page has a "Get a quote …" button under the title and a large link at the end (`.cta`), both to `contact-us/?topic=…#contact-form`; Home has the large link under the tiles. Our Bulgarian wording for them (client's example: "Вземете оферта за електрическа конверсия") sits in `.l10n-bg` spans, like the nav names.
- Footer on every page: offices (London, Sofia head office, Istanbul) + e-mails, fax and a "Contact form" button, then logo + links + "© 2026 ECOES JV". London phone/address and Istanbul phone are left out of the footer until the client sends them (they stay as red "XXX" on the Contact page).
