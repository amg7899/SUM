# ECOES JV website

Static site (plain HTML + CSS + a little JS, no build step) for ECOES JV, built page by page from the client's PDFs in `source/`. Published by GitHub Pages from the branch `claude/website-build-files-m52uk7` (root): https://amg7899.github.io/SUM/

## Working with the client
- The client writes in Bulgarian (sometimes English or Russian); answer in the language of their message. The site is in English (root) and Bulgarian (`bg/`, our translation, added when the client asked that the Bulgarian always say "ECOES конверсия", never the browser's "преобразуване"/"преустройство"). The client reviews the Bulgarian; their wording wins.
- Use the client's text verbatim. Do not add copy, taglines or sections. Fix only obvious typos, and report every fix. When unsure, ask.
- After every change: commit, push to the branch above, and send the live link plus screenshots.
- Photos for the HISTORY section of About us come from https://bulfire.ecoes.eu/about-us/. That host is not reachable from the cloud sandbox, so ask the client to upload them. The client will come back to HISTORY once they have all the information.
- Publishing: GitHub Pages must be switched on by the client (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → this branch, / (root)); no tool here can do it. ecoes.eu currently answers with a certificate for another name (NET::ERR_CERT_COMMON_NAME_INVALID); moving the domain to GitHub Pages later needs DNS changes at the client's registrar.

## Design system (v3, "fresh air": bright, eco, translation-proof)
Subject: an engineering company converting diesel trucks, buses and agricultural/construction machinery to electric. Audience: fleet owners, municipalities, bodybuilders, farmers, investors. The mobile-charging franchise (FRANCHISE ECOES) was removed from this site at the client's request (2026-10-15): it gets a website of its own. Do not bring it back here; once that site exists, a link to it may be added if the client asks. History: v1 light "spec sheet" was rejected as not modern; v2 dark "charged" (chamfered plates, glowing blue traces, Lucide line drawings) was liked for its hero but found too dark. v3 (client's reference: a bright eco landing page with wind turbines, fields, white chrome, lime pill buttons and thin column lines; "bright, modern eco vibe", "professional, not childish/gamecore", "keep the way the website works, just change the theme") keeps the v2 layout, hero composition and every behaviour, and changes only the visual layer.

- Palette (tokens in style.css): haze `#f3f6f1` (page), paper `#fff` (cards, header), sky `#e1ebef` and meadow `#d6e8c1` (the light of the hero, page heads, menu and footer), ink `#14261d` (headings, strong text), text `#34443b`, muted `#56655c` (secondary), line `#dde5da` (hairlines, card edges), line-strong `#c4d0c0` (header frames), field-edge `#84967f` (form fields, 3:1), leaf `#256b40` (links, numbers, focus ring, current page), sprout `#3f9f5f` (drawn lines: hero trace, wires, nodes, markers, underlines), lime `#c8e77d` / lime-deep `#b6dc5c` (buttons) and lime-soft `#e6f3c4` (the closing call to action), signal `#e30613` (logo red: only the € signs and the red "XXX" of missing data), error `#b42318`. All text ≥ 4.5:1 (checked on the rendered pages, gradients sampled).
- Type: Jost (display, 400–700; clean geometric, Futura lineage) + Geologica (text, 300–700, slnt for italics). Both have Cyrillic. Tektur was tried and rejected by the client as "childish, like a video game"; Jost approved and kept in v3.
- Shape: white cards with soft corners (`--radius`, 14–26px) and a 1px `line` edge; one shared rule in style.css (`.plate` and the component list next to it), cards set `--radius`, `--edge`, `--fill`. Buttons are lime pills with the arrow in a dark round chip (`.btn`); the tile and CTA arrows are round chips too. No shadows except a soft lift on hovered tiles and under the dropdown. Thin sprout-green lines act as wiring (no glow): hero trace, About us schematic (trunk, bus, drops; rail with ticks on phones), the dropdown wire, round section nodes on document pages.
- Home: hero with everything centred (client's request): the company name small (in `--text`, not muted, as the globe's lines run behind it), then €CONOMY over €COLOGY huge in ink with red €, without the "&" (client's request; the logo, the page title and the logo's alt text keep it). The two words are set to the same width (in Jost 600 €CONOMY is 0.41em wider: `--track` −0.054em / +0.014em, the trailing letter-space taken back with a negative margin), so both lines start and end flush. The logo's globe is drawn large and faint in green, centred behind the words as in the logo; a green trace draws in once on load (the only non-user motion), centred, at most 1000px wide (with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` its dash covers only 1000px of a longer line). All on the sky-to-meadow light with column lines. Three white tile cards (Electrical Conversion, Bus & trucks, Agro & construction; the franchise tile was removed) with the photo collage, the PDF labels as pale green chips above it, title + lime arrow chip below; hover lifts the card. Three in a row from 1240px; at 860–1239px two, with the third centred under them (`:last-child:nth-child(odd)` spans the row at half width); one column below; the photos keep a common bottom line, so the tiles without chips have white space at the top.
- Document pages: page head on the same light with column lines and a short green line under the title; ruled sections with a round green node, sticky heading column + text column ≥1024px; green diamond list markers; callouts on a pale lime card; the closing all-caps sentence as the big statement.
- Translation-proof rules: no nowrap on body text; containers grow with content; labels never sit on top of photos (chips wrap instead). Header: the menu button (☰) always sits left of the logo and opens every section full-screen; the row of links always stays on ONE line (client's rule): main.js scales its font and gaps down (to 74% at most) until it fits, re-checking on resize, font loads (`loadingdone`, a translation pulls in Cyrillic) and translation (`<html lang>`/class changes, link text changes); below 1024px, or if 74% is not enough, only the menu button remains. On English pages each link (and each "Get a quote" button) carries our Bulgarian name in `span.l10n-bg[lang=bg]` next to `span.l10n-src`, shown via `html[lang|=bg]` in the moment the browser translates to Bulgarian (main.js then moves on to the `bg/` page, see Languages). Brand names, the slogan, e-mails, phone numbers and addresses carry `translate="no"`, with the neighbouring spaces moved inside those spans (translation drops the space next to them otherwise: "ECOESпреустройство").
- Header menu (client's request): Home, About us, ECOES Conversion, Contact us (FRANCHISE ECOES removed, see above), centred in the window (client: "center them … but not to the left"): the row is centred between the logo and the language button, and main.js moves it left (`--nav-shift`, a transform) to the window's middle as far as the free space allows while keeping 48px from the logo — with four links it is exactly centred from 1024px (with longer rows, e.g. translated, it centres between logo and button where the room runs out); "bus & trucks" and "agro & construction" sit in a dropdown under ECOES Conversion (the link itself still opens its page). With a mouse the panel slides down on hover; the arrow button next to the link opens it for touch and keyboard (`aria-expanded`); Escape, a click elsewhere or focus leaving closes it (Escape also hides a hover-opened panel until the mouse leaves). A green wire drops from the link's underline to a white rounded panel (soft shadow) with diamond markers; main.js shifts the panel left (`--sub-shift`) when it would leave the window. In the full-screen menu the two links sit under ECOES Conversion on a green rail. On those two pages the parent link carries the current-page mark (`.is-current`, leaf underline). The footer keeps all six links.
- Test: pseudo-translate every page (Cyrillic, words ~45% longer) at 320/390/768/1024/1440 and check for horizontal overflow and clipped boxes (script used: Playwright, see git history of this note).
- The light and the column lines (replace the v2 Lucide drawings, which were removed with `assets/img/bg/`): the hero, the page heads, the full-screen menu and the footer carry a sky-to-meadow gradient (sky at the top, a sunny haze, meadow green at the bottom; the footer is the meadow). The hero and page heads have thin vertical column lines on the content grid (`::before`, 4 columns, 2 below 768px, `--grid-line` 8% ink, faded at the edges), echoing the client's reference.
- Transparent landscape (client: "add something like transparent features somewhere"): misty hills in layers of translucent white with white edges, a wind farm (five turbines, see-through, ink hairline 20%) and a power line receding towards it; white only, so it lightens the light behind text and never darkens it. One drawing, `assets/img/deco/landscape.svg` (+ `landscape-hero.svg` without the power line), generated by `tools/landscape.py` (edit the turbine/pylon lists there and rerun; turbines keep clear of each other and no blade points straight down). Home has its own symmetrical scene, `landscape-hero.svg`: a wind farm on each side, outside the middle 50% where the centred slogan stands, a low valley under the words, no power line; hero `::after` covers the hero and draws it at the bottom, centred, scaled to the hero's width (at least 560px; layers: column lines −3, globe −2, landscape −1 in the isolated hero). The other places use `landscape.svg`, anchored bottom right: page heads `::after` (beside the title, masked out towards the text, never taller than the head; below 1024px in a band of its own under the button, padding = band + 12px), full-screen menu (background layer), footer `::before` (mirrored, in a band under the last line). Hidden in forced-colors mode. Tried and not chosen: airflow streamlines around the globe, topographic contours, frosted glass panes (empty panes read as placeholders).
- About us HISTORY (after the KINTEX section, as the PDF asks): a `hr.asterism` ("* * *" in sprout green), then `section.history` with the heading, the text and `ul.docs`, a grid of document thumbnails (white cards, one A4-like page shape via `aspect-ratio` + `object-fit: contain` so captions line up, a caption with the document's own title and number below). A thumbnail opens the document full size in a `<dialog class="lightbox">` built by `assets/js/gallery.js` (ink overlay, caption, previous/next, arrow keys, Escape, a click beside the document closes, focus returns to the thumbnail; button labels from `data-close/-prev/-next` on the list, so EN/BG differ only there); without JS the link opens the image. After the grid, `ul.awards`: certificates that each have their own text under them (client: "3 different certs with text under them"), side by side on wide screens (`auto-fill`, 320px min), each with the thumbnail at its own shape (max 340px; the fixed page shape applies to `.docs` only, so lazy images keep the shape of their width/height attributes before they load), a short `h3.doc-title` label from the certificate, then the client's paragraph; with subgrid (as on Contact) the cards in a row take the tallest certificate's height (paper centred) and the labels and texts start on one line; the viewer goes through the documents and the awards together. Images in `assets/img/about/history/` as `name-300` thumbnails and full size (≈600px; the srcset `w` must match the real width), WebP + JPEG; scanner margins are trimmed.
- Photos are being replaced by the client; image frames take any aspect ratio (the tile media flexes, figures are max 960px wide).
- Quality floor: contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible 2px leaf focus ring (outside, following the rounded corners; inside the dropdown items and the e-mail links), 44px targets, WebP + JPEG with width/height, no horizontal scroll at 320–1920px, reduced motion respected.

## Languages (English + Bulgarian)
- `bg/` mirrors every English page: `bg/index.html`, `bg/about-us/`, … with the same markup; only the text differs and asset paths have one more `../`. Every change to an English page must be made on its `bg/` twin too (and vice versa). Links inside `bg/` stay inside `bg/`.
- Terminology (client's rule): "конверсия" everywhere — "ECOES конверсия", "ECOES електрическа конверсия", "конверсия на автобуси и камиони", "селскостопанска и строителна техника"; never "преобразуване"/"преустройство". Also: "вал за отбор на мощност (PTO)", надстройки / производители на надстройки, "Вземете оферта за …". Numbers in Bulgarian style (3,5; 3&nbsp;000; кг, км, об/мин), "EUR" kept. Company and product names stay in Latin (ECOES Ltd., BULFIRE ECOES Ltd., KINTEX SHC, AGRITRUCK); cities in Bulgarian (Лондон, София, Истанбул); the Istanbul and London addresses and the phone placeholders stay as written. Head office: бул. „Ал. Стамболийски“ 205, офис 320А, София 1309, България, ЕС. The CEO: д-р инж. Атанас Гушев.
- E-mail box (client's request): `address.header-mail` left of the language button, a white rounded frame of the same height (46px) with a green envelope and ecoes@ecoes.eu / sales@ecoes.eu one above the other (mailto links, `translate="no"`). main.js hides it (`.mail-off`) when the row of links would not fit at full size with 48px on each side (with the four links it shows at every width from 480px; with longer rows, e.g. translated, it gives way first); with the row off (480–1023px) it shows; below 480px it is hidden (CSS), the footer and Contact page carry the addresses. centerRow() centres the row between the logo and the right-hand group and then on the window, in either direction.
- Language switch: `.lang-switch` at the right end of the header, a frame the size of the menu button showing the other language (BG on English pages, EN on Bulgarian ones). The link to the current language stays in the markup (`aria-current`, hidden by CSS) because main.js reads the page language from it.
- main.js: when the browser translates a page into a language the site has (it sets `<html lang>`: Chrome English → Bulgarian, or Bulgarian → English), it goes to our version of the same page, keeping `?query` and `#hash`. A language chosen with the switch is kept for the tab (sessionStorage `ecoes-lang`); pages in that language then get `translate="no"`, so the browser does not translate them again. Translation into other languages is left alone.
- With `lang="bg"` Geologica uses the Bulgarian Cyrillic letterforms (OpenType locl: т, д, в, и closer to handwriting); Jost has none. The client saw these forms in the translated pages without objecting; standard forms would be `font-feature-settings: "locl" 0` on body (offered to the client).
- Header room: since the dropdown the row of links shows from 1024px in both languages; below that only the menu button.
- To add once the final domain is known: `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en|bg">` with absolute URLs on every page.

## Contact form, calls to action, footer
- Contact form on `contact-us/` and `bg/contact-us/` (`#contact-form`), handled by `assets/js/contact.js`: required name, e-mail, message and consent, errors shown next to each field, subject preselected from `?topic=` (conversion, bus-trucks, agro, other; an old `?topic=franchise` link falls back to the first subject). Its messages follow `data-lang` on the form (en/bg). E-mail subject: "ECOES website: " + the chosen topic (in the page's language). If sending fails, the status says why — "not activated yet" when FormSubmit answers that the form needs activation, otherwise the general error plus a small "Details: …" line — and links to sales@ecoes.eu (cc bulfire@ecoes.eu) with the visitor's entries filled in, so nothing typed is lost. The form keeps its content after a failure. Sent through FormSubmit (no account): AJAX to `https://formsubmit.co/ajax/sales@ecoes.eu` with `_cc=bulfire@ecoes.eu`, `_subject`, `_template=table`, honeypot `_honey`; without JS it posts normally and comes back with `?sent=1`. FormSubmit needs a one-time activation: the first submission sends an "Activate Form" e-mail to sales@ecoes.eu. After activation FormSubmit offers a random alias to replace the address in the form action (hides it from scrapers) — swap it in when the client sends it.
- Every section page has a "Get a quote …" button under the title and a large link at the end (`.cta`), both to `contact-us/?topic=…#contact-form`; Home has the large link under the tiles. The large link says just "Get a quote" / "Вземете оферта" on every page (client: "needs to say Get a quote, not get a quote for el. conv."); About us keeps "Contact our team" (topic other). The buttons under the titles keep "Get a quote for …". Our Bulgarian wording for them (client's example: "Вземете оферта за електрическа конверсия") sits in `.l10n-bg` spans, like the nav names.
- Footer on every page: offices (London, Sofia head office, Istanbul) + e-mails, fax and a "Contact form" button, then logo + links + "© 2026 ECOES JV". London address (sent by the client): 60 Tottenham Court Road, / Office 1980 / Fitzrovia, / London / W1T 2EW, line by line as sent, in Latin on the Bulgarian pages too (`translate="no"`); the first line of the client's block, "ECOES", is left out because the company name stands right above it (reported). London phone and Istanbul phone are left out of the footer until the client sends them (they stay as red "XXX" on the Contact page).
