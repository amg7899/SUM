ECOES JV

ECOES JV (ECO ENGINEERING SYSTEMS Joint Venture) is an association of companies: ECOES Ltd. UK, BULFIRE ECOES Ltd. EU, and ECOES Ltd. Turkey.

  • ECOES Ltd. London

    UK trade company

  • BULFIRE ECOES Ltd. Sofia

    EU manufacturing company

  • ECOES Ltd. Istanbul

    Turkish trade company

The CEO and owner of the JV and all companies within it is Dr. Eng. Atanas Gouchev

Dr. Eng. Atanas Gouchev has 45 years of experience in the automotive industry, comprising 15 years in public administration and 30 years in the private sector.

He holds two Master’s degrees in engineering and a doctorate:

  • Master of Engineering in Internal Combustion Engines,
  • Master of Engineering in the Repair and Operation of Automotive and Tractor Machinery,
  • PhD in the Environmental Aspects of Automotive Engines.

Throughout these 45 years, Dr. Eng. Atanas Gouchev has been involved in the creation and management of complex engineering projects related to the manufacturing and transformation of specialized mobile machinery.

  • ECOES Ltd. (UK) manages UK export and international commercial operations.

  • ECOES Ltd. (Turkey) manages exports, imports, and service operations in Turkey.

  • BULFIRE ECOES Ltd. (Bulgaria, EU) handles manufacturing and service operations, including supplying bare chassis equipped with CNG, LPG, and electric engines, as well as installing and fitting specialized superstructures. The company also converts new and used diesel-powered trucks, buses, and construction and agricultural machinery to run on CNG, LPG, or electric power.

    BULFIRE ECOES Ltd. is a dedicated division of BULFIRE Ltd. established specifically to carry out ECOES conversions. For 30 years, BULFIRE Ltd. has manufactured complex transport vehicles commissioned by institutional clients. Brief information regarding the specialization of the companies operating under the BULFIRE name is provided below:

Strategic Commercial Partnership: KINTEX SHC

https://kintex.bg/ (opens in a new tab)

Through our partnership with KINTEX SHC — Bulgaria’s 100% state-owned foreign trade corporation established in 1966 — ECOES commercial operations benefit from premier institutional credibility. Clients and international buyers can execute procurement and supply contracts either directly through the ECOES JV entities or via KINTEX SHC, ensuring seamless intergovernmental compliance, structured trade finance, and export execution across global markets.
