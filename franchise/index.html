The Franchise Economic Engine: 250% - 400% Guaranteed Margins

Traditional static charging stations are financially trapped by skyrocketing commercial land prices and long administrative delays for high-voltage grid upgrades. The ECOES Franchise Model unlocks massive profitability through strategic energy arbitrage and commercial synergy:

  • Ultra-Low-Cost Charging (The Night Tariff): Your ECOES mobile units are parked at your standard industrial warehouse overnight, charging directly from the grid using cheap night-rate electricity or automated smart-charging contracts during low-demand hours.
  • Premium Energy Reselling (The Peak Highway Margin): During peak daytime transit hours, your mobile hubs are deployed directly to critical highway chokepoints or temporary logistics dead zones where infrastructure is lacking. You sell high-power DC electricity to e-cars and commercial e-trucks and heavy transport fleets at premium highway rates, locking in a massive 2.5x to 3x return per kWh.
  • Synergy Retail Revenue (Dwell Time Economy): Maximize cash flow during the driver's wait time. Factory-integrated, automated vending niches for premium coffee, water, packaged foods, etc., turn your mobile hub into a high-margin, on-the-go retail point, capturing secondary revenue from both EV drivers and passing conventional vehicles.

The 24-Hour Continuous "Shuttle" Model (Zero Asset Downtime)

The ECOES Franchise operations rely on a continuous, high-efficiency "Drop and Hook" rotation utilizing two independent trailer units per operational hub, eliminating the need for heavy machinery, cranes, or forklifts on site:

  • The Cash Generator (On the Road): While Trailer A (over 1000 kWh) is stationed at your designated high-traffic transit point, rapidly fast-charging cars and trucks and generating instant revenue, Trailer B is plugged in back at your industrial base.
  • The Energy Sponge (At the Base): Trailer B absorbs cheap grid power or zero-cost solar energy at a steady, optimized rate without human supervision.
  • The 5-10 Minute Swap: As soon as Trailer A runs empty, the tractor truck brings it back to the base, drops it for recharging, hooks up the fully charged Trailer B, and heads back to the highway. Your business never sleeps, and you avoid all vehicle idle time.
  • The tow truck that swaps the mobile battery modules is also electric. It is a new ECOES electric truck featuring a low price and low operating costs. With a daily range of 500–600 km for replacing the battery modules, the electric tractor ECOES unit will generate savings of 80,000–100,000 EUR per year in diesel and operating costs, directly increasing the franchisee's profit.

Unmatched Financial Performance: about 70 Vehicles Charged per Day

By rotating two over 1000 kWh trailers within a 24-hour cycle, your mobile franchise hub delivers over 1600 kWh of net, usable high-power DC energy daily (accounting for cooling and transfer efficiency).

  • At an average consumption of 30 kWh - 40 kWh per vehicle, an ECOES franchise location grid-services about 70 vehicles every single day.
  • Combined with a 250%–400% price margin and steady secondary vending revenues, the entire system achieves full capital payback in less than 12 months, transitioning into a pure cash-generating enterprise.

With the ECOES Franchise, your location risk is exactly ZERO:

Your asset maintains 100% liquidity, flexibility, and utility.

IMPORTANT FOR SOLAR SYSTEM OWNERS

Individuals and companies that have already built solar parks are forced to sell the electricity they generate to the state or to private energy operators at very low prices. This is due to state regulations governing electricity sales and the use of state infrastructure—such as transmission lines, transformers, and other facilities. Across various countries, average purchase prices for solar energy range from approximately $/€0.04/kW - $/€0.09/KW furthermore, during peak hours, solar energy is often purchased at even lower rates or not purchased at all.

By using the ECOES Franchise, you become independent of state regulations regarding the sale of electricity to the government and its energy infrastructure, as you do not sell electricity to the state or energy operators. Instead, you sell your electricity directly to private individuals.

The prices at which you sell your electricity for vehicle charging will be approximately 4–6 times (400%–600%) higher than the rates at which the state or energy operators currently purchase your electricity. This means that your profit will increase 5 times (500%) on average.
